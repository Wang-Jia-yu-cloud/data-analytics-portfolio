# Olist E-commerce Customer Experience Dashboard

## Executive Summary

This project uses the public Olist e-commerce dataset to explore how delivery performance and product categories affect customer experience.

The goal was not only to review sales performance, but also to understand where customer satisfaction issues may come from and what the business should monitor.

The dashboard was built in Power BI and includes three pages: business overview, delivery and satisfaction analysis, and category-level improvement priorities.

---

## Business Questions

1. What is the overall order, revenue, review, and delivery performance?
2. Are late deliveries linked with lower customer satisfaction?
3. Which product categories should be monitored more closely?

---

## Workflow

* Cleaned and transformed multiple CSV tables in Power Query
* Built relationships between orders, customers, products, order items, and reviews
* Created DAX measures for revenue, orders, review score, low-review rate, late delivery rate, and average delivery days
* Designed a three-page Power BI dashboard for business users

---

## Key Findings

* Late deliveries are strongly linked with lower review scores.
* Late orders have a much higher low-review rate than on-time orders.
* Lower review scores are associated with longer delivery times.
* Some product categories show higher low-review risk, even when they are not the highest-revenue categories.
* Categories with longer delivery times should be reviewed for fulfilment or logistics issues.
